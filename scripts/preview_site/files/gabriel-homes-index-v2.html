[ THE COMPANY ]

New on paper. Deliberate by design.

“Radical transparency isn't a slogan; it's how a listing earns enquiries.” — how we list, in one line

Gabriel Homes was founded in 2026 and makes its home on the Kenyan coast, with an office at Nyali Centre on Links Road, Mombasa. We are a young agency, and we say so plainly rather than dressing the age up. What we lack in history we make up for in how we work: carefully, openly, and with the kind of attention that is easy to promise and hard to fake.

The rule that shapes everything else is simple — nothing goes live unchecked. Every listing on Gabriel Homes passes five separate checks by our team before it appears on the site. The details are verified, the photography is honest, and if something about a property is imperfect, we tell you before you ask. No vague descriptions, no surprises at the viewing.

We serve three areas — Mombasa, Nairobi and Kilifi — and we would rather know three places properly than claim a map we don't. The people who answer your enquiry are the same people who checked the listing, so the person you talk to actually knows the property you are asking about.

[ OUR PROMISE ]

5checks — every listing, no exceptions

Five checks before a listing goes live.

Nothing appears on Gabriel Homes until our team has taken it through five separate checks. The checks happen before publication — not after someone complains — so the site you browse is already the filtered version. What that means for you, in practice:

  • 01

    Verified details

    The facts on the listing — location, size, price, terms — are checked by our team, not copy-pasted from an advert.

  • 02

    Honest photography

    Real photographs of the actual property. If a room is small, the photo will tell you.

  • 03

    A clear word before you ask

    If something is imperfect, we say so up front. No vague descriptions, no surprises at the viewing.

  • 04

    A named person on every enquiry

    Your message reaches the person who checked the listing — with the property details already attached.

We don't publish the checklist item by item — the checks evolve as we learn — but the count is the count: five, every listing, before it goes live.
