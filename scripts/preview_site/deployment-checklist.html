1Executive Summary

The site is functionally complete: every public page, the agent dashboard, the inquiry pipeline and the admin are built and regression-gated. The remaining distance to a live, client-ready deployment is operational, not constructive — environment fixes, four brand-critical polish items, real content, and the production stack itself (hosting, domain, SSL, transactional email). Three of those can only be procured by you; buying them on day one protects the launch date. Everything else needs no further input.
