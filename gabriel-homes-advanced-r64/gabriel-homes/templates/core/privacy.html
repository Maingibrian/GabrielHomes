Your data

Privacy policy.

How Gabriel Homes collects, uses, and protects your personal data — written to comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Gabriel Homes, Kenya

1. Who we are

Gabriel Homes ("we", "us") is a Kenyan real estate agency operating the property portal at gabrielhomes.co.ke. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach our data protection contact through our Contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Enquiry data — the name, email, phone number, and message you submit when enquiring about a property.
  • Saved properties — the listings you save to your account.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and to rate-limit spam.
  • Agent data — agency name, contact details, and bio when you register as a listing agent.

3. Why we use it

We use your data to operate the portal: to show you listings, to pass your enquiry to the relevant agent, to maintain your saved properties, and to protect the site from spam and abuse. We send a newsletter only after at least ten new listings have been published since the last one. Newsletters go only to people who have opted in, and every email carries an unsubscribe link. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

When you enquire about a property, your name and contact details are shared with the agency that listed that property so they can respond. Apart from that, we share data only with service providers who help us run the site (for example hosting and email delivery), under written confidentiality obligations, and only to the extent necessary.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for 24 months so agents can follow up properly. Account data is kept while your account is active and for 12 months afterwards. Server logs are kept for 90 days. After these periods the data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it (in most cases), to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. To exercise any of these rights, use our Contact page — we respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies: a session cookie to keep you logged in and to remember saved-viewing state, and a CSRF cookie that protects forms. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

8. Security

All traffic to the site is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Uploads are validated by type and size, and enquiry and login endpoints are rate-limited against abuse.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will announce it on this page and update the date above. Continued use of the portal after a change means you accept the updated policy.
