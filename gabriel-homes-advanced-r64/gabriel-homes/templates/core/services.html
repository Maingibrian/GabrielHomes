[ 001 — BUYING ]

Find a home to buy.

Browse listings for sale that have passed our checks. Each listing shows the date it was last confirmed.

Enquire on any listing and a person replies. We confirm the price and details in writing with the owner or agent before we show you a property, and we tell you what we checked and what we could not check.

[ HOW BUYING WORKS ]

From first search to a viewing. Your agreement with the seller is between you and them — we recommend a lawyer reviews any contract before you sign.

  1. 1

    Search

    Browse listings that have passed our checks.

  2. 2

    Enquire

    Send an enquiry on the listing. A named person replies.

  3. 3

    Confirm

    We confirm the price and details, and tell you what we checked.

  4. 4

    View

    Arrange a viewing where one is possible.

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit, so you decide with the figure in front of you.

[ 003 — LISTING WITH US ]

List a property with us.

We need written authority from the owner or agent to sell or let the property. The checks on our How we check page run before anything is published — and the photographs we take, or that you give us, are the ones we show. No stock rooms, no borrowed views.

A listing stays live only while its checks are current. When something changes — price, availability, condition — the page says so.

[ 004 — ENQUIRIES ]

Talk to a person about a listing.

Each enquiry goes to a named person who knows the listing. Ask what has been checked, what has not, and when a viewing might be possible.

No call centre, no ticket queue — the reply comes from the same person until your question is answered.

What we will and will not do.

    We will not publish a listing whose checks are incomplete.

  • ii
