[ HOW BUYING WORKS ]

From first search to a viewing.

Your agreement with the seller is between you and them. We recommend a lawyer reviews any contract before you sign.

  1. Search

    Browse listings that have passed our checks.

  2. Enquire

    Send an enquiry on the listing. A named person replies.

  3. Confirm

    We confirm the price and details, and tell you what we checked.

  4. View

    Arrange a viewing where one is possible.

Our fee

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit, so you decide with the figure in front of you.
