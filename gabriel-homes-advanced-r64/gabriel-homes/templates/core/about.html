[ OUR STORY ]

We started Gabriel Homes because property in Kenya too often feels transactional — endless listings, vague details, and agents who disappear once the deposit clears. We wanted the opposite: a small team that talks about the things that actually make a home — the light at four in the afternoon, the street outside, the short walk to the water — and stays around long after the keys change hands.

We are young — founded in 2026, centred in Mombasa — and we are building the collection slowly: homes with character, on streets we would live on ourselves.
