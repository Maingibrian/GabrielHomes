[ OUR COMMITMENTS ]

What you can hold us to.

01

No home goes live unchecked.

If a check cannot be finished, the listing does not make the website — whatever the deadline or the asking price.

02

We say what we could not verify.

Nobody can check everything. When something is unconfirmed, you hear it from us first — not after you have paid.

03

No invented track record.

We will not quote years, sales totals or client numbers we cannot back — we would rather show you our working.

[ QUESTIONS ]

Things people ask us.

Where are you based?

Gabriel Homes is centred in Kenya.

Who pays your fee?

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit.

How do you check a listing?

Gabriel and the team run five checks before a listing is published: ownership, written mandate, site visit where possible, price and details in writing, and encumbrances.

Do you rent as well as sell?

Yes. Rental listings are checked against the rental contract, as well as the other checks.
