[ OUR STORY ]

We started Gabriel Homes because property in Kenya too often feels transactional — endless listings, vague details, and agents who disappear after the deposit lands. We wanted to build the opposite: a small, sharp team that verifies every listing before it goes live, tells you what a place is really like, and stays involved long after the keys change hands. That is what "a human point of view" means to us, and it shapes every part of how we work.

We are a new company, founded in 2026 and centred in Kenya. We are building our listings one checked property at a time, with honest photography. If something is wrong with a property, we tell you before you ask.

We would rather publish fewer homes that we have checked than many that we have not.

If we could not check something, we tell you. Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee, and we tell you the exact amount before you commit to anything.
