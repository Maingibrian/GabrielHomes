Our story

Real estate with a human point of view.

Gabriel Homes is a Kenyan property agency built on a simple idea: people come first, property second. We help you buy, rent, and short-stay across the Kenyan coast and beyond.

We started Gabriel Homes because property in Kenya too often feels transactional — endless listings, vague details, and agents who disappear after the deposit lands. We wanted to build the opposite: a small, sharp team that verifies every listing before it goes live, tells you what a place is really like, and stays involved long after the keys change hands. That is what "a human point of view" means to us, and it shapes every part of how we work.

Today we list {{ listing_count }} live properties across Mombasa, Nairobi, Kilifi, Kwale (Diani), Kiambu and Kajiado, working with {{ agency_count }} partner agencies. Every listing carries verified details and honest photography. If something is wrong with a property, we tell you before you ask — because our work doesn't end when the keys change hands.

What we stand for

Three principles run through everything we do, from the first phone call to the final handover.

[ 001 — People ]

People first, property second.

We start with what you're trying to do — move closer to school, downsize after the children leave, find a place to retire to. The property comes second.

[ 002 — Transparency ]

Radical transparency.

Verified listings, full disclosures, and a paper trail at every step. If something's wrong with a property, we tell you before you ask.

[ 003 — Support ]

Support beyond the keys.

We stay involved after the deal closes — handover, fit-out, connecting you with trusted local trades. The relationship doesn't end at completion.
