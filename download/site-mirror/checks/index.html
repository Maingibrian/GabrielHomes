[ HOW WE CHECK ]

Before a listing goes live

Five checks on every listing.

First we choose a home for how it feels — the light, the air, the street. Then these five checks make it safe to love. If a check cannot be completed, the listing is not published.

← Contact · How we check

[ 001 — LISTING CHECKS ]

What we check.

  1. 01

    Ownership

    For lettings, we check ownership against the rental contract.

  2. 02

    Written mandate

    We obtain written authority from the owner or agent to sell or let the property.

  3. 03

    Site visit

    Where a visit is possible, Gabriel or a team member visits the property and takes on-site photographs.

  4. 04

    Price and details in writing

    Price, size, bedrooms and bathrooms are confirmed in writing with the owner or agent.

  5. 05

    Encumbrances

    We check for caveats, outstanding rates or service charge, and any disputes.

[ OUR COMMITMENTS ]

What you can hold us to.

  1. No home goes live unchecked. If a check cannot be finished, the listing does not make the website — whatever the deadline or the asking price.
  2. We say what we could not verify. Nobody can check everything. When something is unconfirmed, you hear it from us first — not after you have paid.
  3. No invented track record. We will not quote years, sales totals or client numbers we cannot back — we would rather show you our working.

[ 002 — BEFORE PUBLICATION ]

Checked again before it goes live.

  • The five checks above are complete and signed off by Gabriel.
  • Photographs are dated and come from the property itself.
  • The description matches what we checked.
  • The contact and viewing arrangements are confirmed with the owner or agent.
  • Each listing shows the date it was last confirmed.

[ 003 — ASK US ]

Anything we have not checked, we will say.

When you enquire on a listing, ask what has been checked and what has not. You can also ask about the checks before you decide.

Things people ask us.

Where are you based?

Gabriel Homes is centred in Kenya.

Who pays your fee?

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit.

How do you choose which homes to list?

We pick homes for how they feel — the light, the air, the street. Then, before anything goes live, we run five checks: ownership, written mandate, site visit where possible, price and details in writing, and encumbrances.

Do you rent as well as sell?

Yes. Rental listings are checked against the rental contract, as well as the other checks.
