[ HOW BUYING WORKS ]

From first search to a viewing.

Your agreement with the seller is between you and them. We recommend a lawyer reviews any contract before you sign.

  1. Search

    Browse listings that have passed our checks.

  2. Enquire

    Send an enquiry on the listing. A named person replies.

  3. Confirm

    We confirm the price and details, and tell you what we checked.

  4. View

    Arrange a viewing where one is possible.

Our fee

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit, so you decide with the figure in front of you.

Things people ask us.

Where are you based?

Gabriel Homes is centred in Kenya.

Who pays your fee?

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit.

How do you choose which homes to list?

We pick homes for how they feel — the light, the air, the street. Then, before anything goes live, we run five checks: ownership, written mandate, site visit where possible, price and details in writing, and encumbrances.

Do you rent as well as sell?

Yes. Rental listings are checked against the rental contract, as well as the other checks.
