[ OUR STORY ]

We started Gabriel Homes because property in Kenya too often feels transactional — endless listings, vague details, and agents who disappear once the deposit clears. We wanted the opposite: a small team that talks about the things that actually make a home — the light at four in the afternoon, the street outside, the short walk to the water — and stays around long after the keys change hands.

We are young — founded in 2026, centred in Mombasa — and we are building the collection slowly: homes with character, on streets we would live on ourselves.

Things people ask us.

Where are you based?

Gabriel Homes is centred in Kenya.

Who pays your fee?

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit.

How do you choose which homes to list?

We pick homes for how they feel — the light, the air, the street. Then, before anything goes live, we run five checks: ownership, written mandate, site visit where possible, price and details in writing, and encumbrances.

Do you rent as well as sell?

Yes. Rental listings are checked against the rental contract, as well as the other checks.
