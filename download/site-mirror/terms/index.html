The ground rules

Terms of service.

The terms that govern your use of the Gabriel Homes portal — listings, enquiries, accounts, and what we expect from each other.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Gabriel Homes, Kenya

1. Using the portal

Gabriel Homes is a property-listing portal for buyers, renters, and short-stay guests in Kenya. By using the site you agree to these terms. You may browse listings, create an account, save properties, and contact agents for personal, non-commercial purposes. You may not scrape, republish, or resell listing data or photographs without our written permission.

2. Listings and accuracy

Gabriel Homes checks listings before publication and marks checked listings with a "Checked" badge. Owners and agents remain responsible for the information they provide, and we rely on them for some underlying details. Prices, availability, and details can change without notice — always confirm specifics with us before making decisions. Listings are advertisements, not offers or guarantees.

3. Enquiries and dealings with agents

When you enquire about a property, your message and contact details go to the listing agent. Any negotiation, viewing, agreement, or payment happens between you and the agent — Gabriel Homes is not a party to that relationship and does not hold deposits or process rent or purchase payments. Never send money for a property you have not viewed and confirmed with the agent.

4. Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for the activity on your account. Give us accurate registration details and keep them current. We may suspend accounts used for spam, fraud, scraping, or abuse of our enquiry and contact systems.

5. Agent obligations

Agents listing on the portal must own or be authorised to market each listing, must provide accurate details and lawful photographs, and must keep listings current (marking them sold, let, or archived when they are no longer available). We may unpublish listings that breach these rules.

6. Acceptable use

  • Do not submit false, misleading, or unlawful content.
  • Do not use automated tools to access the site without permission.
  • Do not interfere with the site's security features or rate limits.
  • Do not impersonate other people or agencies.

7. Liability

The portal is provided "as is". To the fullest extent permitted by Kenyan law, Gabriel Homes is not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from use of the site, or for the accuracy of third-party listing content. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

8. Governing law

Things people ask us.

Where are you based?

Gabriel Homes is centred in Kenya.

Who pays your fee?

Buyers pay Gabriel Homes a fee. We tell you the exact amount before you commit.

How do you choose which homes to list?

We pick homes for how they feel — the light, the air, the street. Then, before anything goes live, we run five checks: ownership, written mandate, site visit where possible, price and details in writing, and encumbrances.

Do you rent as well as sell?

Yes. Rental listings are checked against the rental contract, as well as the other checks.
